Poshan Saathi — clickable mock-up of a TB-nutrition WhatsApp bot (right). The patient types or sends a voice note of what they ate ("2 roti, dal, rice"); the bot looks up calories & protein, tracks the running daily total, and nudges toward the targets a TB patient needs to recover. The left panel is the adaptive conversation journey, with its clarification and protein-boost branches. Real vs simulated: the food parsing, nutrition values and progress are a front-end mock, not a live model or WhatsApp connection; voice notes are placeholders. Targets — 2000 kcal · 50 g protein/day — are illustrative of adult-TB / Ni-kshay nutrition guidance; a real patient's targets are set by their doctor or dietitian. Built on the ASHA Saathi / HealthBI demo language and interaction patterns.
